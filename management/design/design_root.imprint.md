# IRONAGE.IO DESIGN DOCUMENTATION - ROOT

Openfront-like lightweight game themed on medieval / feudal strategy & diplomacy and subjugating other players as an alternative to conquest,
with multiple paths to any sort of victory the player is aiming for.

The map is built in an Openfront-like way with the use of texelized terrain that is freely conquerable.

The Game focuses on economic buildup in cooperation with other players inevitably leading to inequalities and imbalances, creating conflict. The conflict leads to the creation of vassal chains and internal country politics between players, until the game ends due to one power taking control, or a player manages to become rich enough.

In this folder you will find various documents about the design of specific parts of the game, whether they are mechanics or pure intentions.

# Alpha - CURRENT

Current design aims at providing an "Alpha", a simplified version of the full idea, with fewer features that are still tightly coherent with one another.
Focus: Tight game loop based on a simple economic buildup mechanic (Tax the economy -> Buildup -> Increased Production & Trade <-> Increased population and taxes).
The economic buildup becomes constrained by land and threats from other players (including those who try to speedup their own buildup through raids). It becomes necessary
to establish diplomatic relationship, raise armies, uneven relationships rise until vassalization happens, ensuring the weaker player survives but redirecting some of their
economy to the overlord.
