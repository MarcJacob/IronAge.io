# BACKLOG: Army stance and battle orders

Input date: 2026-10-24

## Item

Basic army actions: stance (settable in or out of battle) and retreat (in battle).

## Description

- Stance: aggressive (bonus attack, malus defense), neutral (none), defensive (bonus defense, malus attack). Strategic choice: aggressive when in a hurry / ignoring losses, defensive to hold an area.
- Retreat: takes a while; flags the army as retreating. In battle during that time: damage dramatically reduced, defense slightly reduced. Then increased travel speed, movement locked until the retreat target is reached. Target chosen by the player when issuing the order (nearest friendly settlement as default).
- Morale (Paradox style): when an army's morale breaks it enters a shattered retreat, taking dramatically more damage. Simple system.
- Related idea: stationing armies inside settlements (not directly targetable, help defend sieges). Design doc currently says armies are bound to their origin settlement and cannot merge into others; garrisoning is not merging, to be reconciled.
