# Army autonomy

Started: 2026-11-08 (planned, not begun, to be done later). Related: `army_supply_redesign_2026-11-07.md`, `alpha_war_extensions_2026-10-18.md`, `match_ai_2026-10-22.md`.

## Scope

Armies become semi-independent agents with self-preservation instead of pursuing their objective directly.

Examples (user):
- Do not helplessly watch themselves run out of supplies.
- Do not attack an enemy army 15 times their size just because they are stationed nearby.

## Direction (user)

- Non-campaign retinues currently must stay in a settlement, join a campaign, or raid (no other supply source).
- Later: armies outside a campaign resupply automatically when low (go to a settlement); may even start raiding on their own when too low.

## Open questions (design first)

- Inputs weighed: supply days left, relative strength, distance to friendly settlement, orders?
- Are orders soft (overridable for self-preservation) or are some hard?
- Campaign's role: sets objectives and supply lines, army decides how?
- Sim-level (also human-controlled armies) or AI-controlled only?
